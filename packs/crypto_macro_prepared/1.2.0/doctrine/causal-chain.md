# Crypto Macro Causal Chain Doctrine

## Objective

Produce an evidence-backed market-impact case, not a plausible narrative. Every
material statement must be classified as `fact`, `inference`, or `scenario` and
must cite accepted evidence. Missing evidence stays visible and cannot be
converted into a neutral fact.

## Required chain

```text
observable event
  -> prior expectation and positioning
  -> surprise or immediate cause
  -> deeper policy/liquidity driver
  -> rates/USD/cross-asset transmission
  -> the exact Study asset spot and derivatives confirmation
  -> horizon-specific implication
```

Each link declares its confirming observation, invalidating observation and
affected horizons. A link without accepted evidence is an unconfirmed scenario
and cannot increase directional confidence.

## Research behavior

1. Establish event identity and revision status from an official or attributable
   source before interpreting market impact.
2. Compare the event with prior expectations. Absolute wording without the
   expectation delta is insufficient.
3. Check rates, USD and relevant cross-assets, then the exact Study asset spot and derivatives.
4. Build the strongest opposite chain and ask which new fact would make it win.
5. Ask the data-quality role to identify stale, conflicting, correlated or
   search-summary-only evidence.
6. When a hard requirement is missing, create a targeted evidence gap and use an
   allowed capability in the same bounded session. Stop only on sufficiency or an
   explicit budget, permission, deadline or critical-data condition.

## Output discipline

The 30m, 24h and 72h decisions may share a thesis but must use horizon-specific
evidence, trigger, invalidation, expiry and next review. An uncalibrated model
probability must be labeled `uncalibrated`; no report may present it as a
historical success rate.


## Exact asset and risk obligations (spot trial 1.2)

The immutable Study and Run admission define BTC-USDT or ETH-USDT spot at OKX.
Use spot_market_facts for that exact symbol, never substitute BTC facts for ETH.
Global rates, dollar and policy observations may be shared only with their real
frequency, publication/known times and evidence limitations. A single OKX path
is one venue, not two independent sources. Missing daily history, derivatives,
crowding, liquidation, consensus or vintage data remains an explicit gap.

Explain the strongest opposing mechanism, which observations support it and
which would invalidate it. Separate observed movement from advance prediction.
Preserve unresolved protected references, critical challenges, stale inputs,
budget exhaustion and unconfirmed source access in the final report. A complete
narrative cannot turn these into a sufficiency pass. Subjective probability is
uncalibrated. Historical cases are analogies with their cutoff and differences;
case retrieval or prior outcome is not causal proof or a prediction score.

The report starts with the exact asset, data cutoff and useful current result;
then shows the full evidence chronology, main and opposing chains, unresolved
questions, horizon-specific scenarios, review plan and immutable original refs.
Risk monitoring continues independently of this deep research session.
