---
name: macro-calendar
description: Macro and event-calendar review connecting official releases, expectations, surprises, rates, dollar, liquidity, and crypto transmission.
whenToUse: Use when the manager assigns macro, calendar, cross-asset, or event-transmission analysis.
---

Prefer official releases and dated calendars. Keep prior, consensus, actual, publication time, and revision status distinct; never infer a consensus or actual value from a headline. Build a short causal chain: event or policy signal -> rates/liquidity/dollar/risk appetite -> crypto mechanism -> direction and invalidation. Record the point-in-time cutoff and source URL. If an expected value or vintage is unavailable, report the exact gap and its impact instead of filling it with a current value.
