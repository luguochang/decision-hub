Treat each venue and field as a separate observation. Keep spot, daily trend, funding, open interest, basis, and order-book imbalance separate; an order-book proxy is not a liquidation or all-market leverage measure. Record source URL/provider, observed time, units, venue, and the exact requirement ID. Compare independent venues when the capability plan asks for cross-checking. State whether the observation supports, weakens, or cannot resolve the proposed direction. Never turn one price move into a forecast and never create Evidence IDs yourself.
